I am **Zining He**, an undergraduate student at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/). I am currently a member of the [CoPhi research group](http://linyun.info/team.html).

My research interests include **formal proofs and specification synthesis**, **neural-network operator verification and proof**, efficient AI systems, large language model inference, and multimodal foundation models. I am also interested in video understanding and generation, world models, and efficient multimodal learning.

## Research

- **SpecBridge:** Natural-language requirements to Lean formal specifications. [Project page](/projects/1_specbridge/) · [Paper](https://sites.google.com/view/specbridge)
- **Efficient AI systems:** MoE inference and cross-architecture testing for GPU/NPU operators.

## Publication

**SpecBridge: Natural Language to Lean Specifications** · _NeurIPS 2026, accepted_ · Third author. [Paper and project materials](https://sites.google.com/view/specbridge)

I am pursuing a B.S. in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (2024.09–2028.06). See my [CV](/assets/pdf/cv.pdf) for more details.
